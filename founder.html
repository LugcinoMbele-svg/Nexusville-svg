Lugcino Ntsuzi

Founder & Chief Executive Officer, Nexusville (Pty) Ltd

Lugcino Ntsuzi founded Nexusville to address South Africa's housing, infrastructure and employment challenges through a different kind of property development — one built around complete, self-sustaining economic ecosystems rather than housing units alone.

Under this leadership, Nexusville's model balances commercial viability with measurable social and environmental impact, guiding the company through its Phase One mandate: proving the integrated development approach at Nexusville Green Village before scaling nationally.
