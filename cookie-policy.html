Cookie Policy

How cookies and similar technologies are used on this website.

Last updated: 14 October 2026

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They are used to make websites work, remember preferences and understand how sites are used.

2. Our use of cookies

This website is a simple informational website. At the time of writing, we do not deliberately set advertising or analytics cookies. If that changes, we will update this policy and, where required, ask for your consent first.

3. Third-party resources

To display the website, your browser may load resources from third parties, for example web fonts from Google Fonts and images from Unsplash. When it does, those providers may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser details, and may set their own cookies in line with their own policies.

4. Managing cookies

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking some cookies may affect how parts of the website display.

5. More information

See our Privacy Policy for how we handle personal information, or contact Lugcino@nomduva.org.za.
