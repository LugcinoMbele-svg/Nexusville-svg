POPIA & Data Protection

Nexusville's approach to compliance with the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013.

Last updated: 14 October 2026

1. Our commitment

Nexusville (Pty) Ltd is committed to processing personal information lawfully and responsibly, in line with the conditions for lawful processing set out in the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA).

2. Information Regulator registration

Nexusville is registered with the Information Regulator (South Africa) under organisation registration number 2026-065568. Our Information Officer is Lugcino Afikile Arnold Ntsuzi and our Deputy Information Officer is Nondumiso Mathe.

3. Compliance framework

Nexusville maintains a POPIA and PAIA compliance framework addressing the protection of personal information, information requests, information security and privacy governance.

4. How we apply the conditions for lawful processing

We aim to:

  • Be accountable — take responsibility for personal information we process.
  • Limit processing — process information lawfully, reasonably and only as needed.
  • Specify purpose — collect information for a specific, explicit and lawful purpose.
  • Limit further processing — not use information in ways incompatible with the original purpose.
  • Keep information accurate — take reasonable steps to keep it complete and up to date.
  • Be open — tell you what we collect and why (see our Privacy Policy).
  • Safeguard information — apply reasonable security measures.
  • Respect your participation — allow you to access and correct your information.

5. Your rights as a data subject

You have the right to be informed about the processing of your personal information; to request access to it; to request correction or deletion; to object to processing; to withdraw consent; and to complain to the Information Regulator.

6. How to make a request

Send your request to our Information Officer. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request, and we will respond within a reasonable time.
