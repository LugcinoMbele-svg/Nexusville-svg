What we build

What can we help you with?

Build from scratch

You own or plan to buy land and want a new house. We record your land, house type, rooms, size, location, budget range and must-have features.

Home rebuild

A damaged or old house, structural redevelopment, a complete rebuild, extra rooms or a new roof.

Home renovation

Kitchens, bathrooms, roofing, painting, flooring, extensions, walls, security, plumbing and electrical work.

Affordable housing

Register your interest in future Nexusville housing developments. Registering does not guarantee a house. It puts you on the list for when a project opens.

Commercial development

Shops, offices, small commercial buildings, community facilities, industrial structures and mixed-use developments.

Green and sustainable building

Solar power and water heating, rainwater harvesting, water tanks, solar lighting, energy-efficient construction and landscaping.

Project support memberships

Structured help before construction starts

Planned monthly memberships. Prices will be published once our costs, services and contracts are finalised.

NexusBuild Starter

Project registration and profile, basic project guidance, document storage, a project dashboard and construction-readiness information.

NexusBuild Project

Project assessment, requirements checklist, budget preparation, document management, contractor coordination and progress tracking.

NexusBuild Development

Development and professional consultant coordination, project reporting, construction management and procurement coordination.

A membership is not payment for construction. It covers project support and preparation. Building work is quoted and contracted separately: quotation, scope of work, contract, payment schedule, then construction.
