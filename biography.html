Personal biography

Lugcino Ntsuzi

I am a South African author and entrepreneur. My journey has been shaped by early hardship, a determination to keep learning, a love of writing and the ambition to build something meaningful.

My beginnings

I was born at General Hospital in Mthatha, in South Africa's Eastern Cape. My roots are in Tsolo, and I spent part of my childhood in Qumbu. My early life was not easy, and some of the experiences that shaped me came long before I was old enough to understand them.

In 2011, when I was five years old, my mother passed away. After her death, my two brothers and I went to live with our maternal aunt, my mother's sister. That living situation became abusive. Social workers eventually intervened and took the three of us to an orphanage, where we stayed for approximately one year before being discharged.

I was still a minor when I later went to live in the village with my grandmother's sisters. Life remained difficult, and my circumstances changed more than once. These are painful parts of my history, but they are also part of the truth of the person I have become. No child should have to experience such hardship. I have had to learn, over time, how to stand up for myself, keep moving forward and take responsibility for the direction of my life.

Education and learning

My schooling began at Zandukwane High School, where I completed Grade 8. I later attended Zimele High School and continued my studies through Grade 11.

My education is not limited to the classroom. I continue to learn through reading, independent study, writing, practical problem-solving and the experience of developing ideas into real projects. I believe that learning is a lifelong responsibility.

My work as an author

Writing became one of the ways I could express ideas, explore human experiences and create something of my own. I have written and independently published books through Amazon Kindle Direct Publishing (KDP). I sell my books for my own profit and continue to regard writing as both a creative pursuit and part of my entrepreneurial journey.

My published titles include:

  • Uhambo: The Journey
  • Twist of Faith
  • Beyond the Shadows
  • The Hell Gatekeeper
  • The Blood
  • Hamlet Hearts

My entrepreneurial journey

My entrepreneurial journey began with ideas. I spent time brainstorming, thinking about what I could build and saving money so that I could register a company. That process taught me that turning an idea into an enterprise requires patience, sacrifice, discipline and a willingness to keep learning.

I have been developing the Nexusville Group vision through my ownership interests in its businesses, including Nexusville Pty Ltd and Nexusville Developments (Pty) Ltd. The wider vision reaches across areas such as infrastructure and property development, information and communications technology, agriculture, renewable energy, manufacturing, logistics and other sectors that can contribute to sustainable development.

I am still building this vision. I see entrepreneurship not simply as registering a company, but as taking responsibility for an idea, learning from setbacks and working towards enterprises that can create opportunities and contribute to communities.

My current personal estimate of my net worth is R4.23 million, reflecting my ownership interests in the Nexusville Group and the value I attribute to my independently published books. This is my own estimate and is not an audited or independently verified valuation.

“Little did I know that the company and its philosophy would sometimes bring me down, yet also lift me higher.”

The values that guide me

Resilience, hope, truth and love are central to the philosophy I try to live by.

Resilience reminds me that difficult circumstances do not have to define the rest of my life. Hope allows me to keep looking towards possibilities that I have not reached yet. Truth calls me to be honest about where I come from, what I have experienced and what I am still learning. Love reminds me to remain human, to value others and to approach life with compassion.

I do not claim to have everything figured out. I am still learning, developing my work and shaping the future I want to create. My journey is ongoing, and I intend to keep moving forward with purpose.
