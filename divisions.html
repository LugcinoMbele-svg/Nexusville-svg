Our operating model

Four strategic divisions. Twelve connected pillars.

Our divisions organise the wider development ecosystem into clear areas of focus. The pillars describe the economic and infrastructure themes that can connect within projects and partnerships.

Division 01

Infrastructure & Property

Residential development

Integrated residential environments and supporting neighbourhood infrastructure.

Commercial & retail

Business premises, trading environments and commercial facilities.

Construction & civil infrastructure

Built-environment development, site infrastructure and enabling works, subject to project approvals and delivery partners.

Division 02

Energy & Utilities

Renewable energy

Solar and other appropriate renewable-energy opportunities and infrastructure.

Water & essential utilities

Water, sanitation and utility systems considered as part of responsible development.

Division 03

Agriculture & Food Security

Agriculture & agro-processing

Productive agricultural infrastructure, processing and local value-chain opportunities.

Food security & rural enterprise

Opportunities that connect local production, enterprise participation and food systems.

Division 04

Business Solutions

Education & skills development

Learning, skills and workforce-development opportunities linked to local economic needs.

Healthcare & community services

Social infrastructure and service-access opportunities.

Tourism & hospitality

Place-based visitor, leisure and hospitality development opportunities.

Logistics & connectivity

Movement, access, distribution and connectivity that support productive places.

Sports, culture & environment

Community facilities, cultural participation, recreation and environmental stewardship.

The twelve-pillar ecosystem is a strategic framework. It does not imply that every sector is currently operational or that a specific project has received funding, approvals or a delivery mandate.
