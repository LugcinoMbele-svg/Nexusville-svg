PAIA Manual

Manual prepared in terms of section 51 of the Promotion of Access to Information Act 2 of 2000 (PAIA).

Last updated: 14 October 2026

1. Introduction

This manual explains how a person may request access to records held by Nexusville (Pty) Ltd, a private body, in terms of the Promotion of Access to Information Act 2 of 2000 (PAIA). The Act gives effect to the constitutional right of access to information held by another person that is required for the exercise or protection of any right.

3. Guide of the Information Regulator

The Information Regulator has compiled a guide on how to use PAIA. It is available from the Information Regulator at inforegulator.org.za.

4. Records available without a formal request

Information published on this website, such as company profile and general information about our services and programs, is available without a formal PAIA request.

5. Categories of records held

Nexusville may hold records in the following categories. Availability is subject to the grounds for refusal in PAIA.

  • company secretarial and corporate records
  • statutory and compliance records (for example CIPC, B-BBEE, tax and POPIA records)
  • financial and accounting records
  • contracts and agreements
  • project and development records
  • correspondence and enquiries
  • policies and procedures
  • information technology and website records

6. Records held in terms of other legislation

Records may be held in terms of legislation applicable to Nexusville, including the Companies Act 71 of 2008, the Income Tax Act 58 of 1962, the Value-Added Tax Act 89 of 1991, the Broad-Based Black Economic Empowerment Act 53 of 2003 and the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013.

7. Processing of personal information

Nexusville processes personal information of enquirers, partners, suppliers and other stakeholders for the purposes described in our Privacy Policy. Personal information may be shared with service providers and as required by law, and is protected by reasonable security measures.

8. How to request access to a record

  1. Complete the prescribed request form (Form 2 of the PAIA Regulations), available from the Information Regulator, and send it to the Information Officer at the email address above.
  2. Provide enough detail to identify the record and the requester, state the form of access required, and explain the right you wish to exercise or protect and why the record is needed.
  3. If the request is made on behalf of another person, include proof of the capacity in which you are making it.
  4. A prescribed request fee may be payable before the request is processed. If access is granted, an access fee may also apply.

We will decide on your request within 30 days of receiving it, unless an extension permitted by PAIA applies. If a request is refused you will be told the reasons and your right to complain to the Information Regulator or to apply to court.
