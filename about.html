About Nexusville

A national development platform, not just a property developer.

Established to meet South Africa's need for sustainable housing, resilient infrastructure and inclusive economic growth.

Company background

Nexusville (Pty) Ltd was founded in response to South Africa's growing demand for sustainable housing, resilient infrastructure, employment creation and inclusive economic development. Rather than focusing solely on residential construction, Nexusville creates complete, self-sustaining economic ecosystems where communities can live, work, learn, invest and prosper.

Our development philosophy combines engineering excellence, environmental responsibility, smart technology and long-term asset ownership, so every project keeps generating value long after construction is complete.

Company purpose

We exist to design, finance, construct, own, operate and maintain integrated developments that improve lives while creating sustainable economic opportunities — from a single flagship pilot to a national network of communities.
