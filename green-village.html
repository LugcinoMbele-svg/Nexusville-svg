Flagship development concept

Nexusville Green Village.

An integrated rural development concept designed to connect essential infrastructure, residential opportunities, productive land use, renewable energy and local enterprise.

The vision

A connected place, not a standalone project.

The Green Village concept explores how a coordinated development can bring together access and internal roads, water and sanitation planning, energy infrastructure, residential development, commercial activity, agriculture and community services.

The intended approach is phased: establish feasibility and land access first, plan essential infrastructure, and then progress viable development components as approvals, funding and partnerships are secured.

Concept components

Potential development elements.

Residential neighbourhood

Housing opportunities integrated with access, services and community facilities.

Commercial & local retail

Trading, service and small-business spaces that can support daily needs and local enterprise.

Renewable energy hub

Potential solar generation and energy services, subject to technical and regulatory feasibility.

Agriculture & food systems

Productive land use and agro-processing opportunities suited to local conditions.

Water, sanitation & roads

Essential infrastructure planning, engineering assessment and implementation sequencing.

Skills & enterprise

Potential local participation, training and business-development pathways.

Project status: development concept. Site selection, land rights, statutory approvals, environmental authorisations, technical studies, funding and implementation arrangements must be confirmed before construction or delivery commitments are made.
