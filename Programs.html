Programs & growth strategy

A phased path from pilot project to national platform.

Nexusville's expansion is deliberately staged — proving the model once, then scaling it responsibly.

Phase One · 2026–2031

Establish & deliver the flagship

Establish the company, complete feasibility studies, secure strategic partnerships, obtain funding, acquire land, and deliver the flagship pilot development: Nexusville Green Village.

Phase Two · 2032–2041

Expand nationally

Expand operations into multiple municipalities across South Africa, diversify revenue streams, strengthen recurring income, and establish Nexusville as a nationally recognised development company.

Long-term vision

A continental network

Build a network of sustainable integrated communities across South Africa, before expanding into selected African markets through strategic partnerships.

Flagship project

Nexusville Green Village

Our pilot development proves the integrated model at community scale — combining housing, agriculture, renewable energy and enterprise in one location before the approach is replicated nationally.
