Privacy Policy

How Nexusville (Pty) Ltd collects, uses and protects personal information.

Last updated: 14 October 2026

1. Who we are

Nexusville (Pty) Ltd ("Nexusville", "we", "us") is a private company registered in the Republic of South Africa under CIPC registration number 2026/260391/07. We are the responsible party for personal information processed through this website (nexusville.co.za), in terms of the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA).

2. Information we collect

We collect only the personal information you choose to give us, for example when you contact us, submit an enquiry or request a service. This may include:

  • your name and contact details (email address, telephone number)
  • the organisation you represent and your role
  • the content of your message or enquiry and any documents you choose to send
  • basic technical information such as your IP address, browser type and pages visited, which your browser or our hosting provider may transmit automatically

3. Why we use it

We process personal information to:

  • respond to your enquiries and requests
  • assess and discuss potential partnerships, investment, supply or collaboration opportunities
  • provide information about our projects and services that you have asked for
  • keep the website secure and working properly
  • meet our legal and regulatory obligations

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for unrelated purposes.

4. Lawful basis

We process personal information where you have consented, where it is necessary to respond to your request or to enter into or perform an agreement, where we are required to by law, or where it is in our legitimate interests (or yours) and does not unreasonably infringe your privacy.

5. Sharing information

We may share personal information only with service providers who help us operate the website, communicate with you or provide professional services, and who are required to protect it, or where we are legally required to disclose it. Where a service provider is located outside South Africa, we will take reasonable steps to ensure the information remains protected as required by POPIA.

6. Security and retention

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against loss, damage and unauthorised access. We keep personal information only for as long as needed for the purpose it was collected, or as the law requires, and then delete or de-identify it. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

7. Your rights

Under POPIA you may ask us to confirm whether we hold your personal information, to access it, to correct or delete it, to object to its processing, or to withdraw your consent. See also our POPIA & Data Protection page. To exercise these rights, contact our Information Officer:

8. Cookies and third-party content

Please see our Cookie Policy for information about cookies and third-party resources used on this website.

9. Links to other websites

This website may link to other websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those sites.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be published on this page with the date it was last updated.
